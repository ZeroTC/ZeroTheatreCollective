OUR PAGE ZERO NEW WORK READING NIGHTS

ARE YOU A PLAYWRIGHT? DO YOU HAVE WORK YOU WANT READ OUT LOUD BY ACTORS?

A few times a year we host reading nights for playwrights to test new work in front of a friendly crowd in a very chilled environment. We're looking for NEW WORK by PROFESSIONAL (or ASPIRING TO PROFESSIONAL) playwrights. Don't know any actors? We'll source 'em. You know heaps of actors and want to use your own? Great!

If this sounds good to you, send us a message on the links below. We usually read 10-15 minute excerpts of plays, but we can go longer or shorter, it's pretty fluid.

We look forward to hearing from YOU!
